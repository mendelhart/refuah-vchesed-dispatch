# Privacy notice draft - not approved for publication

Refuah V'Chesed uses this app to arrange volunteer rides and manage its volunteer roster. It keeps names, phone numbers, ride details and the addresses needed for a ride. Volunteer sign-up may include availability, vehicle details and licence information.

Coordinators and admins see the information needed to arrange rides. Volunteers see the rides offered or assigned to them, not the patient contact list. Admins manage accounts and access. Licence information and stored licence images are encrypted.

Retention periods for removed volunteers' licence images, rejected applications and old trip details have not been approved. The new cleanup for these records is disabled. The office must confirm its retention policy before this wording is published.

For questions, access requests or corrections, contact the office. Before publication, the owner must supply the privacy contact name and contact details, confirm the hosting locations and approved retention periods, and review the wording.

This draft does not replace the currently published notice. No new wording is published until the owner approves it.
