## What this app is for

Refuah V'Chesed uses this app to arrange volunteer rides and other help, and to
manage its volunteer roster.

## What we keep

- Names and phone numbers of the people who call us, and the addresses and
  times needed for a ride.
- Volunteers' names, phone numbers, availability and the services they offer.
- Driver's licence details **only if a volunteer chooses to share them**. They
  are optional. Licence numbers and licence photos are stored encrypted.

## Who can see what

- Coordinators and admins see what they need to arrange rides.
- Volunteers see only the rides offered to them or assigned to them. They do
  not see the list of callers.
- Admins manage accounts and who can see what.

## How we protect it

- Sign-in is by password. **There is no two-step sign-in today.**
- **There are no automatic backups today.** [When backups are set up, say how
  often and where they are kept.]
- At the moment the app does not send text messages, emails or WhatsApp
  messages; messages are recorded for staff only. If that changes, this notice
  will be updated first.
